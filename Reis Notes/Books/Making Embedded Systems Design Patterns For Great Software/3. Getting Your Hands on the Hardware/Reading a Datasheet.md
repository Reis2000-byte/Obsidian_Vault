## Datasheet Sections You Need When Things Go Wrong
- Performance characteristics:
	- Might ignore on first read but when things go wrong making sure that we fall into ranges of things is a good check
- Sample Schematics:
	- Check if the samples are similar to ours

## Datasheet Sections for Software Developers
- Look for a section titled "Application Information" or "Theory of Operation"
- Timing Diagrams are a good spot to stop and digest the information
- Reading a datasheet is a race where the turtle wins over the hare
- Sometimes look at example code or code from other people who have worked with the product
