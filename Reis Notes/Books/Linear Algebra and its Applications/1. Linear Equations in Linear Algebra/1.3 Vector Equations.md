## A Geometric Description of Span{v} and Span{u,v}

- Span {v} is the set of all scalar multiples of v, which is the set of points on the line in R^3 through v and 0
- If u and v are nonzero vectors in R^3, with v not a multiple of u, then Span{u,v} os the plane in R^3 that contains u,v, and 0
