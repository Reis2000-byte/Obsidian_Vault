
## Homogenous Linear Systems

- A system is homogenous if it can be written in the form Ax=0, where A is an m x n matrix and 0 is the zero vector in R^m
- Ax = 0 always has at least one solution x=0
- The zero solution is called the trivial solution and the non trivial solution is a nonzero vector x that satisfies Ax=0

- The homogenous equation $Ax = 0$ has a non trivial solution if and only is the equation has at least one free variable.
